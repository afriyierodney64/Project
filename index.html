1. What the Developer Is Building

The Developer will set up, host, and maintain a custom mobile-friendly web app (PWA) storefront tailored to the Merchant's brand.

1.1 Online Storefront

The platform includes: a product catalogue, a checkout flow, order tracking for customers, and an admin dashboard for managing and fulfilling orders.

1.2 Free POS Add-on

A simple Point of Sale (POS) screen is included as a free bonus for physical shop use. Cash sales made through the POS are completely separate from the online platform and are NOT subject to the revenue split.

2. How Revenue Is Split

Every online payment goes through Paystack (the payment gateway). Here is how the money is divided on each sale:

PartyShareWhat This Means
Merchant 95% Gets 95% of every sale — no deductions at all
Developer 5% Gets 5%, but pays the Paystack fee from it

2.1 Processing Fee

Paystack charges 1.95% of each transaction. The Developer absorbs this fee out of their own 5% cut. The Merchant always receives their full 95%.

3. What Happens with Refunds

If a customer is approved for a full refund, the money is returned to them. Because Paystack does not refund its processing fee, the Developer bears that cost.

3.1 Refund Responsibility

Merchant returns: Exactly what they originally received (95% of the sale price).

Developer returns: Their full 5% commission, absorbing the 1.95% Paystack fee as a loss.

3.2 Example — Full Refund on a GHS 100.00 Order

On a full refund of a GHS 100.00 order, the customer is refunded the full GHS 100.00. The Merchant returns GHS 95.00 (neutral net outcome: GHS 0.00) and the Developer returns GHS 5.00, absorbing the GHS 1.95 Paystack processing fee as a loss (net outcome: − GHS 1.95).
